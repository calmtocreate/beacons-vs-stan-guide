Beacons or Stan Store? Choose the fit, not the hype.

Both platforms help creators organize an online presence. But the right starting point depends on what you're sharing, what you're selling, and what you're ready to spend.

Before you choose

Start with what you need your page to do.

Don't pay for features just because someone else uses them. Think about the next job you need your setup to accomplish.

01

Share useful links

You may mainly need a simple place for resources, recommendations, and your online presence.

02

Sell a first product

You may need a clear product page, checkout, delivery, and a way for interested people to find the offer.

03

Build more workflows

You may want courses, email features, appointments, or additional selling tools as your needs evolve.

Your decision

Three questions worth answering

  • Am I mostly organizing links, or am I ready to sell something now?
  • Would I rather start free, pay a subscription, or compare fees based on expected sales?
  • Which features will I actually use in the next 30–90 days?
Alicia's perspective

My preference isn't your rule.

I've used both Beacons and Stan Store, and Beacons has been my primary platform. That's a reflection of how I've chosen to organize my own online business—not proof that it's the best option for yours.

Look at your workflow, budget, and the experience you want your visitors to have. Choose the fit.
